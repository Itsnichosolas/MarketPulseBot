## 💡 Common questions

**Does the bot execute trades for me?**
No. This tool only provides information and alerts. It never touches your money or your trading accounts.

**Will the bot run if my computer is off?**
No. You must keep your computer on and connected to the internet for the bot to perform its job.

**Is my data private?**
The bot only stores your chosen symbols and your notification preferences. It does not access your personal bank data or private messages.

**What if the alerts stop coming?**
First, check your internet connection. If the connection is fine, restart the application. If the problem continues, log out of your Telegram account within the bot and log back in to refresh the link. 

**Can I stop the bot temporarily?**
Yes. Use the power button inside the application dashboard. This pauses all monitoring without requiring you to close the program entirely.
